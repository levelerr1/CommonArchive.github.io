This is an example article, here to show you how a post with a cover image
looks. Delete this file once you've got real writing to post — or keep it
as a template and just overwrite the text.

A few things worth noticing:

- The circle next to this article's byline is pulled automatically from
  `_data/contributors.yml`, based on the `author:` field above.
- The image at the top came from the `image:` field in the front matter.
- Everything below this line is just normal Markdown.

## You can use headings

Regular paragraphs, **bold**, *italics*, and [links](https://example.com)
all work exactly like you'd expect.

> You can also use a blockquote like this one, if a friend says something
> worth pulling out on its own.

And you can drop an image straight into the body of the article too, not
just at the top:

![A second example image](/assets/images/example-inline.svg)

That's the whole system. Write in Markdown, drop images in
`assets/images/`, and point to them from the front matter or the body.
