I saw a movie monikered *Fractured* a few years back, an American movie where the main
character fought so hard to save his family from an “impending doom”, but if you’ve seen the movie,
you know how that went. But again I saw *Last Straw* by *Tyler Perry* last year, and many brilliantly 
written novels later. There it was staring right into my face—the reality of the mind—, not quite subtle
in fact but yet another reminder.

Don’t get me wrong, I didn’t just realized that the mind is indeed the greatest complexity of God’s creation
as at that moment , but seeing other ways of how it can come into play is quite intriguing, —even made my 
blood jumped in bouts of excitement such that I could feel the thrill down my spine.

And yes, one of the greatest mysteries of the mind is that even the greatest scientists of all time do not 
comprehend the magnanimity of it, crazy, right? I know. For example, in the psychiatry branch of medicine,
there’s no such thing as discharge unlike other aspects where a patient is ruled healthy and can navigate 
life now that they’ve recuperated or beyond the scope of medicine or palliative care thus the end product
being death, there’s only parole, why? The patient is slowly reintroduced into the society, and can relapse
at any time either due to his/her altered mental state or capacity or cannot be considered sane to maintain
the societal standard of living.

![The mind]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/numb.jpg)

Another great example again in psychiatry is a scientist *David Rosenhan* who sent 8 mentally healthy volunteers
to different psychiatric hospitals in the USA in 1973 but were deemed mentally unstable by the hospitals, admitted,
received treatments and given parole. He published it and this shocked everyone on how inaccurate the psychiatric
diagnosis can be and after he announced that he would be sending more, all psychiatric hospitals thoroughly screened
their patients but *David* didn’t send anyone and then they said some were mentally healthy when they were in fact
not, do you get my picture now? I could go on and on why indeed the mind is the craziest concept of the human body 
and its true ability would perhaps not be comprehended.

*“The mind is its own place, and in itself can make a heaven of hell, a hell of heaven.” – John Milton, 
Paradise Lost (1667)*

But I digress, there’s a borderline between right and wrong, between sane and insane and to even be fair, what’s sanity
and insanity in itself? To be honest, no one really knows because the mind is a master trickster. How? I know if you’re
depressed and feel so down that you could feel yourself drawn into utter despair, you want to stay in a dark room and wallow,
you should do the opposite of what you’re feeling, go out, yes. If you’re someone who enjoys dressing according to their mental
state, dress the opposite of how you’re feeling etc. Still doubting? Well, the question is how do you escape from a psychiatric 
hospital if you’re mistakenly taken as a patient? Do you laugh or frown, do you argue or keep calm? The question is endless, 
and you’re never escaping unless they decide according to the *DSM V* manual of psychiatric diagnosis, you deserve parole. 
Now, you get it.

There are different approaches to look at the mind and each offers a different reality: philosophically, scientifically, 
poetically, psychologically, and so on. The mind is absolutely critical, what you see might not be what you see but what your 
mind thinks you should. Do you even think what you’ve read and interpreted exactly what I intended with this article or what 
your mind intended you to?  Do you think you’re really right or wrong in the arguments you’ve had of the life you’ve lived up 
until this moment?

*“The greatest discovery of my generation is that a human being can alter his life by altering his attitudes of mind.”
– William James*.

The mind has its own way and cannot be explained by the simplicity of rational or irrational or right or wrong, it will 
make you do what it only intend and so altering your life comes from the will to alter your mind because the greatest power
we all hold is our mind, now it depends on what you make do with it. And that’s why when you want things enough and believe
you can, even the universe conspire to make you have it.

While the belief that “humans only use 10% of their brain capacity” is false, I honestly believe that is in fact true with 
the mind.
